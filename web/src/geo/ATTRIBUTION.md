# Coastline data

`atlantic-land.json` is a geographic crop of Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons,
clipped to 85°W–25°E and 20°N–85°N and rounded to four decimal places.
Natural Earth data is public domain. The application bundles the crop so the
map works offline; no map tiles or external service are used at runtime.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Retrieved 27 October 2026.
